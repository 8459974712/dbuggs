Frequently Asked Questions
When do I get paid my commission?
Commissions are credited to your wallet within 3–5 business days after the order is delivered. You can request a payout anytime from the Wallet page.
How is my commission rate decided?
Commission rates are based on your dealer tier: Silver (6%), Gold (8%), Platinum (10%). Tier upgrades are done by the admin based on your sales volume.
What is the minimum payout amount?
Minimum payout is ₹100. Payouts are processed via NEFT/RTGS within 1–2 business days.
How do I place a bulk order?
Click "Place Bulk Order" in the sidebar to browse the shop. Dealer pricing and bulk order buttons are shown automatically when you're logged in.
How is TDS calculated?
TDS is deducted at 10% of gross commission as per Section 194H of the Income Tax Act. Net commission = Gross commission − TDS.
